### Resolution of Failed Edge Cases
1. **Quoted Local Parts with `@` Symbol**: The splitting logic was corrected to split on the rightmost `@` rather than enforcing a strict count of one `@` across the entire string. This allows addresses like `"user@name"@example.com` to parse successfully.
2. **Whitespace Stricteness**: Auto-stripping was removed. Whitespace at the boundary of a string is now treated as structurally invalid.
3. **Double Hyphens in Domain Labels**: Enforced the RFC 5890 constraint that rejects double hyphens in the 3rd and 4th positions of a domain label (e.g., `ex--ample.com`) unless prefixing an IDN A-label (`xn--`).
4. **IP Literal Support**: Enabled parsing of standard IPv4 and IPv6 domain literals enclosed in brackets (e.g., `user@[127.0.0.1]`).
